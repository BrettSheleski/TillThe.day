- **Set the trip's dates.** With the last day as the end, the countdown keeps going during the trip ("happening now") instead of stopping when you leave.
- **Put the destination in the location** for a map link everyone can tap.
- **Use the notes for the essentials**, like "Flight UA 123 · Hotel check-in 3 PM".
- **Share it with everyone going**, or add it to your phone's home screen to check on it at your desk.
