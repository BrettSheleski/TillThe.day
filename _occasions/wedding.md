- **Add the venue as the location.** Guests get a map link on the countdown, and the address goes into their calendar when they add it.
- **Use the notes for the details guests ask about**, like "Black tie · RSVP by May 1".
- **Add an end time** for when the reception wraps up. On the day, the countdown switches to "happening now" until then.
- **Put it everywhere you share news:** the family group chat, your wedding website (Share, then *Embed on a website*), or the QR code on your save-the-dates.
- **On the day**, open it full screen on a TV at the venue for the last few minutes.
