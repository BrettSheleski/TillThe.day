- **Add the arrival time and place**, like the airport or base, so family get a map link and can add it to their calendars.
- **Keep it to what you'd share publicly.** Anyone with the link can read it, so leave out details like unit movements or flight numbers you'd rather keep private.
- **Make it big on the day:** open it full screen on a TV for the last hour.
- **Count the time apart, too.** Once someone has left, a countdown with their departure date counts up from it.
