- **Enter the date of birth, not this year's date.** It counts down to the next birthday and shows which one it is, like "30th".
- **One link works every year.** After the day, it moves on to the next birthday by itself, so there's nothing to update.
- **Add it to your calendar** and it repeats every year (with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook's .ics file; Outlook.com adds just the next one).
- **Having a party?** Make a separate countdown for it with the time and address, so guests can add it to their calendars.
- **Counting down to someone else's birthday?** Change the message, like "Mom's birthday", and send them the link.
