- **Pick the due date and leave the time empty.** It counts down to the start of that day wherever each person is.
- **Due dates move.** If yours changes, make a new countdown and share the new link; the old one keeps its date.
- **Think about what goes in the message.** Anyone with the link can read it, so leave out a name or anything you're not sharing yet.
- **Share it with grandparents-to-be**: they can add it to their calendar, or add the page to their phone's home screen to check in on it.
- **Planning a baby shower?** Make a second countdown for it, with the time and address, so guests can add it to their calendars.
