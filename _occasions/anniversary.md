- **Enter the original date, not this year's.** The countdown works out the next anniversary and its number by itself.
- **Plan a surprise?** Make it for your partner, add a message like "Another year of us", and send it the morning of.
- **Booked a dinner?** Make a second countdown for that evening with the time and the restaurant as the location, so the map is one tap away.
- **One link lasts for years.** After the day, it moves on to the next anniversary, so it's worth saving to your phone's home screen.
