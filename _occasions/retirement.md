- **Add the time you finish**, like 5:00 PM on your last day, to count down to the very minute.
- **Share it with coworkers**, or add it to your phone's home screen to check on it between meetings.
- **Planning a send-off?** Make a second countdown for the party with the time and place, so everyone can add it to their calendar.
- **After the big day**, make a countdown with your last day as the date: once a date has passed, it counts up, showing how long you've been retired.
