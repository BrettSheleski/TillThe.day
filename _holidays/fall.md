Astronomical fall, or autumn, begins at the **September equinox**, around September 22 or 23, when day and night are roughly equal everywhere. This countdown runs to the exact moment of the equinox, worked out from the Earth's orbit, shown in your own time zone.

South of the equator, the September equinox starts spring.
