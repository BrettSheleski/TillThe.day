Astronomical summer begins at the **June solstice**, around June 20 or 21, the longest day of the year in the Northern Hemisphere. This countdown runs to the exact moment of the solstice, worked out from the Earth's orbit, shown in your own time zone.

South of the equator, the June solstice starts winter.
