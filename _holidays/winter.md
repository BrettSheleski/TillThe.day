Astronomical winter begins at the **December solstice**, around December 21 or 22, the shortest day of the year in the Northern Hemisphere. This countdown runs to the exact moment of the solstice, worked out from the Earth's orbit, shown in your own time zone.

South of the equator, the December solstice starts summer.
