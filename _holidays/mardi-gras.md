Mardi Gras, or Fat Tuesday, is the day before Ash Wednesday and the start of Lent, **47 days before Easter**. Because Easter moves, Mardi Gras falls anywhere from February 3 to March 9. It's famous for the carnival celebrations in New Orleans.
