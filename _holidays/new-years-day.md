New Year's Day falls on **January 1** every year, marking the start of the new calendar year. Many people celebrate the night before, counting down the final seconds to midnight on New Year's Eve.

This countdown runs to midnight on January 1 in your own time zone, so it reaches zero right as the new year begins where you are.
